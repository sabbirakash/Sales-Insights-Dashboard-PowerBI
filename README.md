# 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Insights Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution built using **SQL**, **Power BI**, **Power Query**, and **DAX**.

The dashboard analyzes sales transactions across multiple markets, customer types, and time periods to provide actionable insights into revenue, profitability, customer performance, and sales trends.

It enables business stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify high-performing markets, evaluate customer contributions, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track revenue and profit trends
- Analyze market-wise profitability
- Compare customer types
- Identify top-performing customers
- Evaluate monthly sales trends
- Build an interactive executive dashboard

# Key Business Insights

## 1. Strong Revenue Performance

The business generated **984.81M** in total revenue from **2.43M sales quantity**, demonstrating strong sales activity.

---

## 2. Healthy Profit Margin

A total profit margin of **24.66M** provides visibility into overall business profitability.

---

## 3. Revenue Trend Analysis

Revenue fluctuates across different months and years, helping management identify seasonal demand patterns and periods of declining performance.

---

## 4. Market Performance

Profit contribution varies significantly by market, allowing decision-makers to identify the most profitable business regions.

---

## 5. Customer Segment Analysis

Brick & Mortar customers contribute the majority of total revenue, while E-Commerce represents a smaller but important share.

---

## 6. Top Customers

A small number of customers contribute a significant portion of total revenue, highlighting key strategic business relationships.

---

## 7. Interactive Time Intelligence

Year and month filters allow business users to quickly compare sales performance across different time periods.
